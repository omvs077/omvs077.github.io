ByteLock Support

Help for installing, using and uninstalling ByteLock. Contact: dvvyomlabs19@zohomail.in

System requirements

Windows 10 (version 1903 or later) or Windows 11, 64-bit. Free disk space of roughly the size of your largest locked folder is needed when unlocking.

Installing

  1. Download ByteLock-Setup.exe from the download page and run it. ByteLock installs for your own Windows account, so no administrator rights are needed.
  2. Windows warning. Windows may show "Windows protected your PC / Unknown publisher". This happens because the installer isn't signed with a paid code-signing certificate. Click More info, then Run anyway.

First-run setup

On first launch you'll create your Master Recovery Key. Write it down and keep it safe. It's your only way to get your data back if you forget a folder password, and it's needed to uninstall. A short welcome guide explains the basics.

Locking a folder

  • Right-click a folder and choose Lock with ByteLock, or use Lock a Folder in the app.
  • Enter a password. Large folders show a progress bar.
  • Afterwards, the folder is replaced by a lock-icon placeholder named <folder>.blocked, and a hidden encrypted container <folder>.blk appears.

Unlocking a folder

  • Double-click the .blocked placeholder and enter your password, or use Unlock a .blk Container in the app.
  • The folder is restored to its original place, and the placeholder, container and recovery file are removed.

Forgot a folder password?

Open Settings → Master Recovery, choose the folder's placeholder, and enter your Master Recovery Key.

FAQ

Why does Windows say “Unknown publisher”?

The installer isn't signed with a paid certificate. Use More info → Run anyway.

What happens if I lose my Master Recovery Key and my folder password?

Your data can't be recovered, by us or anyone else. That is how zero-knowledge encryption works. Keep the recovery key somewhere safe.

It says “Wrong password, or the container is damaged.”

ByteLock can't tell a mistyped password from a damaged container, because both fail the same integrity check. Check your password first. If you're sure it's right, the container may be damaged (for example by an interrupted copy or a disk error). Damaged containers can't be repaired. Restore the file from a backup if you have one.

Why am I asked to wait after wrong passwords?

After a few wrong guesses (3 free attempts), ByteLock adds a growing wait of up to 5 minutes. It resets after about 10 minutes without attempts. This protects you against guessing.

I can't see my .blk or .blk_recovery files.

They're hidden on purpose. In Explorer choose View → Show → Hidden items. Don't delete, rename or move them: keep the .blk, .blocked and .blk_recovery files together.

Can I lock a folder with Hindi, emoji or other non-English characters in its name?

Yes. Unicode folder names are supported.

Is my folder safe while it's unlocked?

No. An unlocked folder is ordinary, unprotected files. ByteLock protects locked folders only, and can't protect against malware or keyloggers on your PC.

Can antivirus flag ByteLock?

Unsigned encryption tools can sometimes trigger a generic warning. If this happens, confirm the file's checksum and report it to us so we can look into it.

How do I uninstall?

Start Menu → ByteLock → Uninstall (or run Uninstall.exe in the install folder). You'll be asked for your Master Recovery Key. ByteLock unlocks the folders it is tracking, then removes itself.

Unlocking fails with a message about disk space.

Unlocking restores the full folder while the container still exists, so you need free space about equal to the folder's size.

Reporting a problem

Email dvvyomlabs19@zohomail.in with:

  • your Windows version (press Win+R, type winver),
  • your ByteLock version (Settings → About),
  • the exact text of any error message, and what you were doing.

Never send your password, Master Recovery Key, or a .blk file. We'll never ask for them.
