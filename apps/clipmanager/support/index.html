Common questions

?

Where is my data stored?

Locally, in %APPDATA%\ClipManager\. See the privacy policy for details.

?

Can I change the Ctrl+Shift+V shortcut?

Not in this version — it's fixed to avoid clashing with per-app shortcut customization. This may change in a future release.

?

How do I remove my history?

Open Settings from the tray icon → History tab → Clear All History.

?

How do I uninstall it?

If installed from the Microsoft Store: Start menu → right-click ClipManager → Uninstall. This also removes its local data.
