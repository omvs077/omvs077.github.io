How do I open my clipboard history?

Press Ctrl+Shift+V anywhere in Windows. The history popup opens with your most recent clips at the top.

Where is my history stored?

Entirely on your own machine, at %APPDATA%\ClipManager. Nothing is uploaded anywhere — see the privacy policy for details.

Can I stop it from capturing something?

Yes — pause monitoring from the tray icon at any time, or turn on password-manager exclusion in Settings → Privacy so those apps' clipboard content is never captured.

How do I change how much history is kept?

Open Settings → History to set your history limit, enable auto-delete, or turn on clear-on-exit.

The tray icon disappeared / the app won't open.

Check Task Manager for a running ClipManager.exe process. If it's not there, relaunch the app from the Start menu. If the issue persists, email us with your Windows version and we'll help track it down.

How do I fully remove ClipManager and my data?

Uninstall the app as usual, then delete the %APPDATA%\ClipManager folder to remove your stored history and settings.
