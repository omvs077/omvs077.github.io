What it does

⚡

Instant history, anywhere

Ctrl+Shift+V opens your recent clips over whatever app you're in — no switching windows first.

🖼

Text, images, and files

Captures more than plain text — screenshots, copied images, and files or folders dragged from Explorer.

📌

Pin what matters

Keep frequent clips at the top of the list so a busy day of copying doesn't push them out.

✎

Snippets

Save reusable text — signatures, boilerplate, commands — separately from your everyday clipboard history.

🔍

Search as you type

Find something you copied minutes or days ago without scrolling through the whole list.

🔒

Built with privacy in mind

Excludes password managers by default, and you can pause monitoring or clear your history at any time.

Shortcuts

The ones you'll actually use.

Ctrl+Shift+V   Open clipboard history
Ctrl+P   Pin the selected clip
Ctrl+Del   Remove the selected clip
Ctrl+N   New snippet
Ctrl+E   Edit the selected snippet
