ClipManager

A clipboard history manager for Windows — save, search, and reuse everything you copy.

Get it on Microsoft Store Privacy Policy

What it does

Save everything

Text, links, colors, emails, file paths, and images — automatically detected and kept in one searchable history.

Quick search

Find any past clip in seconds, right from the history popup.

Pin & organize

Pin clips you reuse often so they never fall out of history.

Image support

Copy an image and get an instant thumbnail preview, ready to paste back anywhere.

Privacy controls

Exclude password managers, pause monitoring anytime, or clear history on exit.

Lightweight

Built in native C++/Win32 — no Electron, no .NET, no background bloat.

Quick shortcuts

  • Ctrl+Shift+V — open clipboard history
  • Enter — paste selected clip
  • Ctrl+P — pin / unpin
  • Del — remove clip
  • Esc — close history popup
