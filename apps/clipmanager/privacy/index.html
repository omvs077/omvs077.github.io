Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

ClipManager is built to work entirely on your own machine. This page explains exactly what that means.

What ClipManager stores

ClipManager keeps a history of what you copy on your Windows PC: text, images, and references to files or folders you copy in File Explorer. This can include anything on your clipboard, so treat it the same way you'd treat your clipboard itself — it may contain sensitive information depending on what you copy.

Where it's stored

Everything is saved locally, in %APPDATA%\ClipManager\ on your own device: your clip history, saved images, and your settings. Nothing is uploaded, synced, or transmitted anywhere. ClipManager makes no network requests, has no account or sign-in, and includes no analytics or telemetry.

Password managers

By default, ClipManager skips saving anything copied while a recognized password manager is the active window. You can turn this off in Settings, though we'd recommend leaving it on.

Your controls

From Settings, you can:

Clear your entire history at any time
Pause clipboard monitoring
Turn off image or file capture
Set history to auto-delete after a number of days
Clear history automatically whenever ClipManager exits

Uninstalling

If you installed ClipManager from the Microsoft Store, uninstalling removes the app and its local data together. If you used the standalone installer, your data in %APPDATA%\ClipManager\ remains on disk until you delete that folder yourself.

Children

ClipManager isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone — it doesn't collect anything from anyone, since nothing leaves your device.

Changes to this policy
