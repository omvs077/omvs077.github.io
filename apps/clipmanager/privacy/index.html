Privacy Policy

ClipManager · Last updated: September 2026

ClipManager stores everything entirely on your own device. It does not send your clipboard data, or any other data, anywhere.

What ClipManager stores

  • Your clipboard history (text, links, colors, emails, file paths) — saved locally at %APPDATA%\ClipManager\history.txt
  • Thumbnails/copies of images you copy, saved locally alongside your history
  • Your app settings (history limit, hotkeys, appearance, etc.) — saved locally at %APPDATA%\ClipManager\settings.txt

What ClipManager does not do

  • No network connection of any kind — ClipManager works fully offline
  • No account or sign-in required
  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting sent off-device
  • No data is shared with, or sold to, any third party — there isn't a server to share it with

Your controls

  • Exclude password managers from clipboard capture, in Settings → Privacy
  • Pause monitoring at any time from the tray icon
  • Set history to clear automatically on exit
  • Delete individual clips, or clear your entire history, at any time from the popup

Uninstalling

Uninstalling ClipManager removes the app. To also remove your stored history and settings, delete the %APPDATA%\ClipManager folder.
