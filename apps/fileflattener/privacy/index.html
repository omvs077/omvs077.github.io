Privacy Policy

FileFlattener · Last updated: August 2026

FileFlattener does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data, usage analytics, or telemetry. It does not connect to the internet or any remote server during operation.

Local data

FileFlattener reads and writes files only within folders you explicitly select, for scanning, deduplication, and ZIP export. It stores one small local settings entry, via Windows QSettings under HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\FileFlattener, containing:

  • Your 5 most recently opened project folder paths
  • Interface preferences

This never leaves your device and is not accessible to Dvvyom Labs or anyone else.

Third-party services

FileFlattener does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or cloud services.

Children's privacy

FileFlattener does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it collects no data at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, an updated version will be published at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
